Policy Name: Flexible Hybrid Work & "Focus Friday" Policy
Policy ID: HR-2026-05
Effective Date: April 1, 2026
Version: 1.2
1. Purpose
[Company Name] aims to foster a productive, balanced work environment that supports employee wellbeing while ensuring operational excellence. This policy outlines guidelines for our hybrid work model and the implementation of "Focus Fridays" to reduce meeting fatigue.
2. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees whose job functions can be performed remotely.
3. Hybrid Structure (3/2 Model)
Office Days: Employees are encouraged to work from the office at least two (2) days per week to support collaboration and team culture.
Remote Days: Employees may work remotely up to three (3) days per week.
Core Hours: Regardless of location, all employees are expected to be available for collaborative work between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM EST.
4. "Focus Fridays"
To allow for uninterrupted deep work and project completion, [Company Name] implements "Focus Fridays."
No-Meeting Zone: No internal meetings should be scheduled on Fridays.
Exceptions: Urgent client meetings or unexpected emergencies.
Communication: Internal chat (Slack/Teams) status should be set to "Focus Mode" on Fridays.
5. Remote Work Requirements
Employees working remotely must ensure:
A secure, confidential, and professional working environment.
Reliable high-speed internet connectivity.
Adherence to all company IT security protocols, including the use of company-provided VPN.
6. Compensation and Benefits
Hybrid work arrangements do not change employee salary, benefits, or work responsibility requirements.
